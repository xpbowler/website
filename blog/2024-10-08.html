A Note to Myself

2023-10-08


  1. Ignore advice if it doesn't apply to you.
  2. It's okay to be extreme sometimes.
  3. Don't chase nostalgic feelings.
    You are a different person than five years ago. Would you really feel the same way doing the same things you did back then? Isn't it better to discover new pleasures?
  4. It's your life. Who cares what others think.
    Harder said than done.
  5. Practice gratitude.
  6. Build good habits.
    You're not him. You can't wake up one day and 180. Slow motion is better than no motion.
  7. Eat healthy, exercise, and journal.
  8. The money will come back.
  9. Never give up.
    Yeah no shit. Being delusionally self confident is okay. It gives you the best shot at succeeding. After a setback, you have no choice but to keep going. Otherwise, you are guaranteed to fail.
  10. Moderation and balance.
    e.g., you care about both money and passion to some degree. Don't pretend you don't. Find the balance that matches your values and priorities. A lot of advice is extreme and too generalized.
  11. Everything happens for a reason. Even bad things.
    You will become the person you were destined to become.
  12. Never look back.
    Of course, in moderation. Look back to learn lessons from mistakes, but never worry about what could've been. Look outwards and forwards. You are a different person than the you who made that decision.
